AI and formalization·Nov 11

yang-mills-group posts OpenAI construction of pure Yang-Mills in Lean

The construction, obtained October 28, 2026, proves a challenge statement with a Mathlib-only import closure: for each closed matrix subgroup with nonabelian simple real Lie algebra, a Wilson lattice family with a Wightman reconstruction (positive energy, unique vacuum). A kernel replay found only standard axioms; a positive mass gap is outside the challenge's scope.

AI and formalization·Nov 11

Crowd-sourced project aims to make the 4D Yang-Mills Lean proof human-readable

The hairer/humanise-yang-mills repository plans a research monograph giving detailed proofs, traceable to a fixed version of the announced Lean formalisation of four-dimensional Yang-Mills theory, with contributors' agents working on tasks claimed through GitHub issues. Preliminary dependency mapping is under way; a full build, axiom audit, and line-by-line mathematical audit are still outstanding.

AI and formalization·Nov 10

With LongTails, AI agents prove more than 100 new Lean-verified optimization bounds

In the Lean 4 framework by Alexander Tyurin, QuocViet Pham, Valentin Khrulkov, and Ivan Oseledets, agents such as Codex or Claude Code search for better oracle-call budgets under a fixed problem setup, and Comparator independently audits each result. One new gradient-only guarantee for (L0,L1)-smooth convex optimization resolves what the authors call an open problem.
