## Headlines

- **Polona Durcik registers AI-generated Lean proof on triangles in positive-density sets.** The Palomar registry entry formalizes the theorem that every Lebesgue measurable planar set with positive upper Banach density contains a translated and rotated copy of every sufficiently large dilate of any fixed noncollinear triangle. The proof was developed using GPT 6 Astra Pro with human oversight; Claude Opus 5.5 Medium generated the Lean formalization.
