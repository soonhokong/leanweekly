Lean core·Oct 15

Lean now ships verified kernels (proof checkers) and a lake check command

Leo de Moura writes that AI agents are effective at finding vulnerabilities in Lean's kernel. Lean v4.35.0's lake check rechecks a project with leanchecker, the official Lean kernel without the baggage around it, and lake check --paranoid runs four additional kernels, including con-leche, the first kernel for Lean written in Lean and proven consistent.

Papers and events·Oct 16

Jeremy Avigad and coauthors back formalizing all of mathematics, if done well

The Xena guest post, co-written by Kevin Buzzard, Johan Commelin, Rémy Degenne, Kim Morrison, Oliver Nash and Adam Topaz, follows a big OpenAI release and warns against building formal libraries nobody uses. It highlights Tau Ceti, an AI-written library slightly larger than Mathlib, and says most of mathematics can be formalized within three years.

AI and formalization·Oct 16

Shanghai AI Lab's Shusheng-S2 helps prove 3D Ghys conjecture, verified in Lean

Tech Times calls Shusheng-S2 the first Chinese AI model to help resolve a world-class open conjecture in pure mathematics: the three-dimensional Ghys conjecture, unresolved for 31 years. A Peking University team, using up to 100 AI agents, independently verified every logical step of Jiesong Zhang's proof, producing more than 550,000 lines of Lean code.

AI and formalization·Oct 18

Researchers formalize Razborov's flag algebra method for extremal graph theory in Lean

Gyeongwon Jeong, Seonghun Park, Jihoon Hyun, Sang-il Oum, and Hongseok Yang cover finite simple graphs in Lean 4, building on Mathlib, and add a tactic that turns semidefinite programming certificates into Lean proofs. Results include seven Turán-type upper bounds and the first formalization, to the authors' knowledge, of the asymptotic Erdős pentagon theorem.

⊢Checked against: arXiv:2607.23500

AI and formalization·Oct 15

AxiomProver generates Lean proof of Collatz positive density results

CollatzPosDens, a Palomar registry entry, formally proves the main results of Kenny Lau's paper "Explicit positive lower density for the Collatz Conjecture", including Theorem 1.3, an explicit positive density of odd starting values reaching a prescribed target not divisible by 3. AxiomProver, Axiom Math's in-house theorem proving system, generated the Lean files.

⊢Checked against: palomar-registry.org·AxiomMath/CollatzPositiveDensity
