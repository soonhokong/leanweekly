Leo de Moura speeds up equation generation for match on many literals

With 64 literal patterns, generating equation theorems (which simp [f] and cbv need) used to hit the heartbeat limit. Leo de Moura fixed this with a series of pull requests; one makes equation generation for a String → Nat function with 128 literal alternatives and a catch-all about 20% faster.

Mac Malone lets Lake packages declare their own cache services

Services are specified in a cache_service TOML table or Lean DSL declaration and can be used via lake cache get --service. A new downloadCacheService package option sets the service Lake uses by default for lake cache get of the package and, when the artifact cache is enabled, for on-demand downloads during builds.

Lean core·Nov 7

Sebastian Ullrich speeds up deriving Repr, BEq, and Hashable on mutual blocks

Lean now derives these classes once per mutual block, generating the auxiliary functions once for the whole block instead of once per type. This removes quadratic elaboration cost: deriving Repr on every type of a 76-type mutual block went from 309 seconds and 10.2 GB to 62 seconds and 1.3 GB.

⊢Checked against: lean4#16339

Papers and events·Nov 18

Xueying Qin, Marco Peressotti and Fabrizio Montesi mechanise asynchronous choreographies in Lean

The paper presents Nyarlathotep, the first asynchronous nondeterministic choreographic programming framework, and proves that endpoint projection, which compiles a global program into network programs, is sound and complete and yields deadlock-free networks. The Lean 4 mechanisation takes about 20,000 lines using CSLib, compared with about 40,000 for Mech, the less featureful calculus Nyarlathotep extends.

⊢Checked against: arXiv:2611.24649·paperswithlean.com

AI and formalization·Nov 14

Teng Zhang proves Schmeisser's 1977 conjecture; OpenAI's Codex formalizes it in Lean

The conjecture strengthens Sendov's: if a polynomial's zeros lie in the closed unit disk, every point of their convex hull is within distance 1 of some critical point. The Mathlib-based formalization covers every degree with no sorry placeholders or extra axioms, and Lean's kernel checks its interval certificates for degrees 4 to 6,000.

⊢Checked against: paperswithlean.com·zhangteng2000/schmeisser-conjecture

Rylan Malarchick presents qlin, a dynamic quantum circuit compiler with Lean proofs

Lean 4 proofs show that qlin moves operations out of measurement-dependent branches without delaying the program, and that its check of each compiled program against its source is sound. Modeled at the QICK controller's 42 ns per decision, 10 of 65 programs run at least 10% faster than the best baseline, up to 91.2%.
