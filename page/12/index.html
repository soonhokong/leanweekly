Lean core·Feb 15

Leo de Moura adds higher-order Miller patterns to grind's e-matching

A merged Lean 4 pull request lets grind's e-matching engine keep lambda arguments as patterns when pattern variables are applied only to distinct lambda-bound variables. Previously such lambdas were ignored during matching, which limited theorems about combinators like List.foldl that take a function argument.

⊢Checked against: lean4#12483
