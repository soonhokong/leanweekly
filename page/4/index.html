Coding agents autoformalize a norm-variation result for multiple ergodic averages in Lean

Floris van Doorn, Polona Durcik, Joris Roos, Lenka Slavíková and Christoph Thiele released a blueprint and Lean 4 formalization of a quantitative strengthening of Tao's norm-convergence theorem, answering an open question of Avigad and Rute. Vasily Ilin runs a collaborative Lean 4 formalization of Mazur's torsion theorem

Lean core·Aug 20

Lean 4 redefines Decidable as a Bool paired with a proof

A pull request by Robin Arnez, merged on 2026-08-20, changes Decidable p to a structure containing a Bool and a proof of either p or ¬p, giving more definitional equalities and letting the decide tactic stop carrying proofs. The breaking change was possible now that the new compiler is enabled.
