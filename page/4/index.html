Leo de Moura prototypes on-demand commutation theorems for state operations

Leo de Moura's Commute repository combines a simproc (a metaprogram that simp calls on matching subterms) with realizable constants, so only the pairs proofs use are ever proved, avoiding a quadratic table of lemmas. Floris van Doorn, Polona Durcik, Joris Roos, Lenka Slavíková and Christoph Thiele released a blueprint and Lean 4 formalization of a quantitative strengthening of Tao's norm-convergence theorem, answering an open question of Avigad and Rute.
