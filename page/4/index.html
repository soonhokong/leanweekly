Verification·Sep 24

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory formalizes a metrology quantity calculus in Lean 4

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory's PropertyKindCalculus formalizes René Dybkær's ontology on property, extended with Flater's tracking of kinds of quantities, and is described as the first in which the quantity calculus itself, beyond the taxonomy around it, is a checked artifact.
