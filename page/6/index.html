Lean core·Jul 22

Patrick Hulin reports a kernel check gap allowing a proof of False

In leanprover/lean4#14484, Patrick Hulin reports that the kernel does not reject free variables in opaque declaration bodies, letting an adversarial metaprogram prove False with no axiom dependencies. The report, largely generated with AI, suggests applying the closure check used for definitions and theorems, and the issue is now closed.
