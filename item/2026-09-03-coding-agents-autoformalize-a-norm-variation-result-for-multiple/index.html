Coding agents autoformalize a norm-variation result for multiple ergodic averages in Lean

Floris van Doorn, Polona Durcik, Joris Roos, Lenka Slavíková and Christoph Thiele released a blueprint and Lean 4 formalization of a quantitative strengthening of Tao's norm-convergence theorem, answering an open question of Avigad and Rute. The proof was autoformalized during a one-week period using coding agents under human supervision, producing roughly 107k lines of code.
