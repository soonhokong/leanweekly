Machine-checked Lean 4 proof of the general Courtade–Kumar inequality released

A repository by @dpwoodru contains a complete, machine-checked Lean 4 proof of the general Courtade–Kumar inequality (the most informative Boolean function conjecture), formalizing the paper by Z. Chen, A. Gohari, A. Javanmard, H. Lin, V. Mirrokni, C. Nair and D. P. Woodruff. The whole proof spans 45,500 modules.
