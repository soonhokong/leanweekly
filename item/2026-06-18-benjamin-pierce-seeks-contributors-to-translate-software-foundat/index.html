Benjamin Pierce seeks contributors to translate Software Foundations to Lean

Software Foundations, a series of textbooks on formal proof and foundations of programming languages formalized in Rocq, is being translated to Lean for Fall courses at Penn and elsewhere. Benjamin Pierce says the team is ready to add a few people, especially educators, and will need alpha-testers later in the summer.
