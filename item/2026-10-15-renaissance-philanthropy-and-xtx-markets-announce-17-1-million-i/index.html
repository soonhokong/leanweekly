Renaissance Philanthropy and XTX Markets announce $17.1 million in AI for Math grants

The AI for Math Fund selected 22 applications representing 30 organizations from more than 470 submissions, bringing its total commitments to $35.1 million. Winners include TorchLean, a Lean-native framework for verified machine learning, and a project turning a Lean 4 visual proofwriting prototype into open-source infrastructure for reading AI-generated proofs.
