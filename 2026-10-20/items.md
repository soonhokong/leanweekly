## Headlines

- **Joris Roos formalizes OpenAI's L³ trilinear Hilbert transform bound with coding agents.** The Lean 4 formalization, about 194,000 lines of code, verifies the main result of a preprint OpenAI made public on October 6, 2026 without an accompanying formalization. The theorem statement is human-written, while Codex and Claude coding agents, following a blueprint generated by GPT-6 Astra Pro, autoformalized the proof over about eight days.
