Lean core·Jun 13

Lean 4.31.0 released with new do loops, Lake linting, mvcgen'

Lean 4.31.0 lands 305 changes, including while let loops in do blocks, a built-in Lake linting framework, stricter transparency handling in definitional-equality checking, and the experimental mvcgen' tactic, which can outperform mvcgen by over 100x on some synthetic benchmarks. The bundled toolchain moves from LLVM 19 to LLVM 22.

⊢Checked against: v4.31.0 release notes·lean4 v4.31.0

Kevin Hartnett publishes The Proof in the Code, a book about Lean

Quanta Books published The Proof in the Code on June 9, 2026, in which Kevin Hartnett tells the story of the birth and rise of Lean, from a bug-checking program at Microsoft Research developed by Leo de Moura to its adoption by mathematicians and tech firms.

Papers and events·Jun 17

Benjamin Pierce seeks contributors to translate Software Foundations to Lean

Software Foundations, a series of textbooks on formal proof and foundations of programming languages formalized in Rocq, is being translated to Lean for Fall courses at Penn and elsewhere. Benjamin Pierce says the team is ready to add a few people, especially educators, and will need alpha-testers later in the summer.
