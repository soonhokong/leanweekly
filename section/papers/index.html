Renaissance Philanthropy and XTX Markets announce $17.1 million in AI for Math grants

The AI for Math Fund selected 22 applications representing 30 organizations from more than 470 submissions, bringing its total commitments to $35.1 million. Winners include TorchLean, a Lean-native framework for verified machine learning, and a project turning a Lean 4 visual proofwriting prototype into open-source infrastructure for reading AI-generated proofs.

Papers and events·Sep 21

Paper ports Iris, a concurrent separation logic framework, to Lean

Markus de Medeiros, Ralf Jung, Lars Birkedal and nine coauthors explore how Lean features like flexible metaprogramming and quotient types can simplify the design and usage of Iris, providing a new implementation of the Iris proof mode with improved performance. Integration with Mathlib supports an application to probabilistic program verification.

Papers and events·Aug 31

Four new arXiv papers settle open problems with Lean 4 formalizations

Qianli Ma solves Colombo's 1928 determinant problem, Jef Pauwels proves the Pál and Vértesi conjectures on the I3322 Bell inequality, Zijun Gao and Weihan Zhang falsify an n^(-1/4) near-optimality conjecture from a ChatGPT 5.6 Pro proof attempt, and Gregory Morse shows a multiplicative complexity of exactly nine.

Papers and events·Aug 29

Maher Kallel and Mohamed El Louadi measure the audit burden of AI proofs

The paper studies ten results an OpenAI lab published in August 2026 with Lean 4 certificates: kernel-checked proofs total 20.6 MB against 55.6 KB of statements needing human audit (379 to 1), yet those statements contain 218 bespoke definitions. Four weeks later, one result remained disputed over whether its formalization meant what it claimed.
