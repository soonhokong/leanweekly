Papers and events·Oct 22

Leo de Moura publishes MiniForth, a Lean tutorial for programming language semantics

MiniForth's Forth-inspired teaching language, whose programs act on a stack, has arithmetic, DUP, SWAP, DROP, calls, and conditionals. The tutorial embeds it as Lean functions (a shallow embedding) and as data (a deep embedding), proves program equivalences, and adds a second pass where grind handles routine reasoning; the build checks the examples and proofs.

Paper ports Iris, a concurrent separation logic framework, to Lean

Papers and events·Aug 29

Maher Kallel and Mohamed El Louadi measure the audit burden of AI proofs

The paper studies ten results an OpenAI lab published in August 2026 with Lean 4 certificates: kernel-checked proofs total 20.6 MB against 55.6 KB of statements needing human audit (379 to 1), yet those statements contain 218 bespoke definitions. Four weeks later, one result remained disputed over whether its formalization meant what it claimed.
