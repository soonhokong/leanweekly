Lean FRO and ICARM open Palomar, a registry of Lean-verified mathematics

AI and formalization·Aug 26

MathAdv benchmark and ProofEvolve prover target Lean 4 theorem proving

Jiaxin Yuan and coauthors introduce MathAdv, a diagnostic benchmark spanning 13 domains across undergraduate- and graduate-level mathematics, finding formalization remains a major bottleneck. AI and formalization·Aug 4

LeetProof is an agentic pipeline built on Velvet, a multi-modal verifier embedded in Lean, that generates a program, its formal specification, and a machine-checkable proof from a natural-language description. AI and formalization·Apr 21

New arXiv papers test LLM formalization faithfulness, agents, and benchmarks in Lean

Kyuhee Kim, Auguste Poiroux and Antoine Bosselut evaluate GPT-5 and DeepSeek-R1 on 303 first-order logic problems: GPT-5 fabricates axioms during proof generation, while DeepSeek-R1 mistranslates premises.
