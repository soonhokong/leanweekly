Ziqing Xiang formalizes Haemers' conjecture for the generalized spectrum in Lean

The Mathlib-based formalization contains the accompanying paper's whole proof that the proportion of n-vertex graphs determined by their generalized spectrum (the spectra of the adjacency matrices of a graph and its complement) tends to 1 as n grows. Haruhisa Enomoto formalizes two classical theorems of Bautista, Gabriel, Roiter and Salmerón

Built on Mathlib, the complete formalization follows Haruhisa Enomoto's new proof that representation-finite algebras over algebraically closed fields have normed multiplicative bases (closed under products up to zero) and basic ones in characteristic other than two are standard (indecomposable modules match their Auslander–Reiten quiver's mesh category). It also proves the Auslander–Reiten theory it uses.
