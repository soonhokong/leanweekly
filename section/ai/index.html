Researchers formalize Razborov's flag algebra method for extremal graph theory in Lean

Gyeongwon Jeong, Seonghun Park, Jihoon Hyun, Sang-il Oum, and Hongseok Yang cover finite simple graphs in Lean 4, building on Mathlib, and add a tactic that turns semidefinite programming certificates into Lean proofs. Results include seven Turán-type upper bounds and the first formalization, to the authors' knowledge, of the asymptotic Erdős pentagon theorem.

AI and formalization·Sep 20

Ten Claude Opus 5.5 agents produce a Lean-verified shortest-path algorithm, C-HD

Geby Jaff of Vals AI had 10 Claude Opus 5.5 agents coordinate on a message board; after about 15 hours and 733 messages they proposed C-HD, whose Lean proof (checked with the Lean Comparator tool) establishes a strict asymptotic improvement over n log n for a stated density of edges. The constants are enormous, so no practical speedup is established.

LeetProof is an agentic pipeline built on Velvet, a multi-modal verifier embedded in Lean, that generates a program, its formal specification, and a machine-checkable proof from a natural-language description.
