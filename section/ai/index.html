Among four-qubit pure states, only M₄ and its complex conjugate, up to local unitaries, maximize the mean von Neumann entropy of the three balanced bipartitions; the paper also proves the Gour-Wallach conjecture for Rényi entropies. The Lean 4 and Mathlib formalization covers most four-qubit results and parts of the computer-assisted seven-qubit ones.

Murali Menon proves Herzog-Schönheim conjecture for solvable groups, with Lean formalization

The Herzog-Schönheim conjecture states that if a group is partitioned into finitely many, and at least two, cosets of subgroups, then two of these subgroups have the same index. The arXiv paper proves it for all solvable groups, finite or infinite, and the proof has been formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib.
