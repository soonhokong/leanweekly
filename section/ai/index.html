Google's launch post for Gemini 4 Argon (dated 2026-10-13) reports an arcsin implementation, a building block in recent quantum simulation algorithms, written by Argon and formally verified in Lean. It cuts spacetime cost by more than 100x compared to a published quantum CORDIC baseline.

AI and formalization·Oct 8

AI proof-search agent resolves nine open Erdős problems, verified in Lean

A paper in Science by George Tsoukalas, Swarat Chaudhuri and coauthors describes an agent for formal proof search that autonomously resolved nine of 353 open Erdős problems and proved 44 of 492 On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences conjectures. A basic agent alternating LLM-based generation with Lean-based verification replicated the Erdős successes.

AI and formalization·Sep 27

Four authors formalize the Hamilton-Perelman proof of the Poincaré conjecture in Lean

Ziyang Qin, Yuan Liao, Ayush Khaitan, and Bennett Chow formalize the smooth three-dimensional Poincaré conjecture via Ricci flow with surgery and finite-time extinction. Together with the Moise smoothing theorem, the result yields the topological three-dimensional Poincaré conjecture.

⊢Checked against: arXiv:2609.33842

AI and formalization·Sep 20

Ten Claude Opus 5.5 agents produce a Lean-verified shortest-path algorithm, C-HD

Geby Jaff of Vals AI had 10 Claude Opus 5.5 agents coordinate on a message board; after about 15 hours and 733 messages they proposed C-HD, whose Lean proof (checked with the Lean Comparator tool) establishes a strict asymptotic improvement over n log n for a stated density of edges. The constants are enormous, so no practical speedup is established.

⊢Checked against: vals.ai

AI and formalization·Sep 4

Anthropic's Claude formalizes a complete proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean

Working largely autonomously over 11 days, Claude wrote 13 million lines of Lean (over 5x the size of Mathlib, the principal community library) and proved 29,500 intermediate theorems. Kevin Buzzard compiled the code and ran comparator on it, and the full proof is on GitHub with a written walk-through.

Floris van Doorn, Polona Durcik, Joris Roos, Lenka Slavíková and Christoph Thiele released a blueprint and Lean 4 formalization of a quantitative strengthening of Tao's norm-convergence theorem, answering an open question of Avigad and Rute. AI and formalization·Aug 26

MathAdv benchmark and ProofEvolve prover target Lean 4 theorem proving

Jiaxin Yuan and coauthors introduce MathAdv, a diagnostic benchmark spanning 13 domains across undergraduate- and graduate-level mathematics, finding formalization remains a major bottleneck.
