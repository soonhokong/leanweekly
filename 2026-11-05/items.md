## Headlines

- **Researchers formalize the Strong Perfect Graph Theorem in Lean with AI agents.** The complete Lean proof follows, result by result, the 2006 proof by Maria Chudnovsky, Neil Robertson, Paul Seymour and Robin Thomas that a finite graph is perfect (every induced subgraph's chromatic number equals its clique number) exactly when it is Berge (it and its complement lack induced odd cycles of length at least five). ([mahdi-jfri/StrongPerfectGraphTheorem](https://github.com/mahdi-jfri/StrongPerfectGraphTheorem), [palomar-registry.org](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-11-06-000006&version=1))
