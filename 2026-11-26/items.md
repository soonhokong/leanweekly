## Headlines

- **Yaoling Yang formalizes the existence part of Zauner's conjecture in Lean.** The proof shows that symmetric informationally complete POVMs (SIC-POVMs) exist in every finite dimension, settling Google DeepMind's formal-conjectures statement and eleven open benchmark cases. It follows D. Radchenko and C. Wheeler's argument, with a reduction by M. Appleby, S. T. Flammia and G. S. Kopp, and is independent of Māris Ozols's earlier formalization. ([palomar-registry.org](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-11-26-000040&version=1), [Hertaspace/sic-povm-lean](https://github.com/Hertaspace/sic-povm-lean))
