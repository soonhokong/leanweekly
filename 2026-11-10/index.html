Verification·Nov 10

Google DeepMind uses AI agents to verify Signal's messaging security in Lean

The researchers used AlphaProof Code, a multi-agent framework for formal software specification and verification, on Signal's post-quantum PQXDH handshake and Triple Ratchet. The agents generated 6.5 million lines of Lean code proving the libsignal implementation correct against a human-reviewed specification; the proof assumes perfect cryptography.

AI and formalization·Nov 10

Google researchers use AI agents to design Lean-verified quantum programs

Google DeepMind and Google Quantum AI researchers had teams of AI agents write quantum programs in a Lean library and prove both correctness and resource cost; a separate checker confirmed each solution met the original specification. The preprint reports a 290-fold reduction over prior baselines for a quantum arithmetic routine (an arcsine calculation).

AI and formalization·Nov 9

Project Numina formalizes a braided fusion category preprint in Lean 4

The preprint, by Justin Asher, Jonah Berean-Dutcher, Clay Cordova, Julio Parra-Martinez and Richard Tuddenham, proves that every non-degenerate pivotal braided fusion category with property F is pseudo-unitary and weakly integral, assuming neither sphericality nor pseudo-unitarity. The formalization also includes the extension to degenerate categories under a positivity hypothesis.
