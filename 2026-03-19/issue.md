## Headlines

- **Math, Inc.'s Gauss autoformalizes Viazovska's 24-dimensional sphere-packing proof in Lean.** Gauss, a Math, Inc. reasoning agent built to formalize proofs automatically, produced over 200,000 lines of Lean code for Maryna Viazovska's 24-dimensional proof in two weeks, without a preexisting blueprint. The work follows the 8-dimensional formalization announced on February 23, which built on the Formalising Sphere Packing in Lean project. ([spectrum.ieee.org](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-proof-verification))
- **Axiom raises $200 million to verify AI-generated code with Lean.** Axiom Quant's Series A round, led by Menlo Ventures, values the startup at $1.6 billion. Axiom trains AI systems to generate formally verified outputs in Lean and plans to scale its training infrastructure and expand its team of math experts. ([siliconangle.com](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/12/verifiable-ai-startup-axiom-raises-200m-prove-ai-generated-code-safe-use/))
- **Mistral AI releases Leanstral, an open-source code agent for Lean 4.** Mistral AI released Leanstral, with 6B active parameters and weights under an Apache 2.0 license. On FLTEval, a new suite built from pull requests to the FLT project, Leanstral pass@2 scores 26.3, beating Sonnet by 2.6 points while costing $36 to run versus Sonnet's $549. ([mistral.ai](https://mistral.ai/news/leanstral))
- **Renaissance Philanthropy and XTX Markets add $13.5 million to AI for Math Fund.** The new commitment brings the AI for Math Fund total to $31.5 million, with grants ranging between $100,000 and $1 million to support 12-24 months of work. Applications close on March 30, 2026 at 11:59 pm PT. ([renaissancephilanthropy.org](https://www.renaissancephilanthropy.org/insights/renaissance-philanthropy-and-xtx-markets-additional-13-million))
