Levent Alpöge releases a Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, working with Claude, released the proof on November 20, 2026, as a Lean library comprising 17 million lines of code; an unmodified mirror is on GitHub. AI and formalization·Nov 19

AlphaProof Nexus finds Lean proof of high-dimensional photonic GHZ no-go theorem

Mario Krenn and coauthors used AlphaProof Nexus, which combines language models with Lean compiler feedback, to prove a Formal Conjectures statement: no complex-weighted monochromatic quantum graph exists for any even N of at least 4 when D is at least N. Generating an N-photon, D-dimensional GHZ state is equivalent to constructing such a graph.

Verification·Nov 18

Long Qian and André Platzer check ODE reachability proofs with Lean 4

The paper's differential dynamic logic proofs certify ordinary differential equation (ODE) reachability, like UNSAT proofs in satisfiability solving, and any approximation correctly computed via Taylor models (a technique for rigorous ODE bounds), even by untrusted algorithms, provably yields one. The checker, implemented in Lean 4 for improved performance, can formally verify ARCH competition benchmarks.

⊢Checked against: arXiv:2611.25303

Verification·Nov 19

Tether monitors AI agents with security policies written in Lean 4

Tether, by Meghana Aparna Sistla and coauthors, sandboxes proposed commands and commits side effects only if a policy allows them; its Lean 4 DSL is both the executable monitor and the specification for mechanized proofs. On coding-agent benchmarks, one shared policy blocks 100% of in-scope attacks with a 4.7% benign false-positive rate.

⊢Checked against: arXiv:2611.25599·paperswithlean.com

AI and formalization·Nov 20

Harry Wang formalizes the Four Color Theorem in Lean 4 without Mathlib

The self-contained proof follows Georges Gonthier's hypermap framework and the published mathematics of Neil Robertson, Daniel P. Sanders, Paul Seymour, and Robin Thomas. It verifies all 633 unavoidable configurations (249 D-reducible, 384 C-reducible) and all 5 cartwheel discharging trees directly inside the Lean kernel without native_decide.

AI and formalization·Nov 19

Hannah Cairo and five coauthors formalize Mizohata-Takeuchi power-loss theorem in Lean

The theorem states that for every smooth hypersurface whose second fundamental form is nonzero at some point, the local Mizohata-Takeuchi constant grows at least like a power of the radius, with the exponent chosen before the dimension and the surface. The AI-assisted formalization, registered in the Palomar registry, passed the official Lean Comparator.

⊢Checked against: palomar-registry.org·VjekoKovac/lean-mt

GPT-6.2 Astra formalizes Agata Smoktunowicz's gap theorem in Lean 4

Working alone, the model wrote about 1,800 lines of Lean in 94 minutes; Vals AI reports no other model on the Autoform-Hard leaderboard solves this task. AI and formalization·Nov 19

Zayn Blore formalizes Charles J. Read's counterexample to the invariant subspace problem

Following Wiesław Śliwa's 2010 explicit version, the proof shows there is a bounded operator on the sequence space ℓ¹ that maps no closed subspace, other than 0 and the whole space, into itself. Drafted with Claude Code, it proves Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures statement Invariant_subspace_problem_l1; the open separable Hilbert space case is not addressed.

Verification·Nov 13

Daksh Shami and Charles Yuan verify MARBLE's rewrite rules in Lean 4

The MARBLE language compiles publication-style matrix formulas into quantum circuits, applying algebraic rewrite rules that decompose each matrix into simpler matrices with known circuits; Lean 4 proofs show every rule preserves matrix semantics. MARBLE matches or surpasses 37 of 39 published baselines and produces exponentially smaller circuits than the leading tool FABLE.

⊢Checked against: arXiv:2611.17756·paperswithlean.com
