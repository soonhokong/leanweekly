Teng Zhang proves Schmeisser's 1977 conjecture; OpenAI's Codex formalizes it in Lean

The conjecture strengthens Sendov's: if a polynomial's zeros lie in the closed unit disk, every point of their convex hull is within distance 1 of some critical point. The Mathlib-based formalization covers every degree with no sorry placeholders or extra axioms, and Lean's kernel checks its interval certificates for degrees 4 to 6,000.
