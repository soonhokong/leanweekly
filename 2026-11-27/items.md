## Headlines

- **Robert D. Barish refutes Dürer's conjecture with a computer-assisted Lean 4 proof.** An explicit convex polyhedron with 276 vertices and 548 triangular faces, squeezed flat enough, cannot be cut along edges and unfolded into the plane as one piece without overlap. The proof, which also refutes two flat variants about squeezed polyhedra, rests on an exhaustive machine search whose certificate the Lean kernel replays. ([RBarish-UTokyo/Durer-Counterexample-Lean4](https://github.com/RBarish-UTokyo/Durer-Counterexample-Lean4), [palomar-registry.org](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-11-27-000021&version=1))
