## Headlines

- **Anthropic posts claimed VP ≠ VNP proof by Claude, formalized in Lean.** The paper, credited solely to Claude, claims a proof of Valiant's VP ≠ VNP conjecture (an algebraic analogue of P versus NP) over every field. Anthropic's README states that Lean checks the proofs, but no machine checks that the formal statement says what is claimed, and no human mathematician has checked the proof. ([casrai.org](https://casrai.org/news/anthropic-claude-authored-vp-vnp-proof-unverified-lean-2026))
- **OpenAI publishes preprint and Lean proof claiming VP and VNP differ.** OpenAI added the preprint to its openai/math repository on November 23, 2026, alongside LaTeX sources, a PDF, and a Lean proof. It claims a superpolynomial arithmetic circuit lower bound separating VP (efficiently computable polynomials) from VNP (NP-like polynomials in the algebraic setting), as Valiant conjectured in 1979; expert acceptance is still open. ([explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/openai-vp-vnp-different-lean-proof-november-2026))
- **AI agents formalize the proof of Tadeusz Iwaniec's 1982 conjecture in Lean.** Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Opus 5.5, at the request of The-Anh Vu-Le, formalized every result of Andrea Agazzi et al.'s preprint on the L^p norm of the Beurling–Ahlfors transform (a singular integral), plus the cited results the paper uses. The Lean and NanoDa kernels accept the proofs, which no person has reviewed. ([vltanh/lean4-iwaniec](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-iwaniec), [palomar-registry.org](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-11-25-000005&version=1))
