## Headlines

- **Zayn Blore proves in Lean that the Poisson conjecture is false.** Following the explicit counterexample in Christopher D. Long's unrefereed 2026 preprint, the proof shows the conjecture (every substitution preserving the canonical Poisson bracket is invertible) fails for two canonical pairs and hence for every number of pairs from two up. It proves four statements from Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures, with their definitions copied verbatim.
