## Headlines

- **Wolfram Institute publishes reference Lean environment for Rule 30 Prize submissions.** The verifier checks every submission against exactly this Lake project (Lean 4.35.0, Mathlib's v4.35.0 tag), and the submissions site has instructions for AI agents. The kit contains the official statements of Problems 1 and 2, proved partial results, 163 equivalences and 49 sufficient conditions for Problem 1, and 14 equivalent forms of Problem 2. ([WolframInstitute/Rule30PrizeKit](https://github.com/WolframInstitute/Rule30PrizeKit))

## Verification in practice

- **Anand Kumar Keshavan announces lean-qverify for verifying quantum circuits in Lean 4.** The open-source project includes a Lean library, a Qiskit integration, and a free workbook with executable examples covering circuit specifications, semantic proofs, Quantum Hoare Logic and ZX-calculus. Anand Kumar Keshavan calls the current implementation an initial research and educational framework, welcomes independent technical reviews, and is interested in collaborating on compositional proof techniques. ([akkeshavan/lean-qverify](https://github.com/akkeshavan/lean-qverify), [leanprover.zulipchat.com](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/579630-Project-announcements/topic/lean-qverify.20.E2.80.94.20open-source.20formal.20verification.20for.20quantum.20c/with/634576355), [akkeshavan.github.io](https://akkeshavan.github.io/lean-qverify/))
