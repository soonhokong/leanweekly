AI and formalization·Jan 11

Axiom's AxiomProver solves all 12 Putnam problems with Lean proofs

AxiomProver, an autonomous AI theorem prover built by Axiom, solved 12 out of 12 problems from the Putnam Competition, 8 of them within the strict exam time limit, producing formal proofs in Lean. One combinatorial step in Problem A5 required 2,054 lines of formal proof.

⊢Checked against: LinkedIn post

AI and formalization·Jan 9

Hacker News discusses Erdos problem #728 solved largely autonomously by AI

A Hacker News thread on the claim that Erdos problem #728 "was solved more or less autonomously by AI" drew 619 points and 363 comments. A Harmonic employee said Aristotle, the company's system, can translate an informal English proof into Lean, and commenters debated how to check that a theorem statement is formalized correctly.

⊢Checked against: news.ycombinator.com

Lean core·Jan 10

Sebastian Ullrich proposes bundling Lean's Init library into one .olean file

A pull request by Sebastian Ullrich bundles all Init module files into a single file with cross-sharing, reducing cumulative size from 325MB to 264MB and openat calls for import Init on Linux from about 2000 to about 80. The pull request is still open.
