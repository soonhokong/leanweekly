Verification·May 5

Jonathan Protzenko uses Aeneas to verify bounds safety in jxl-rs

Jonathan Protzenko's case study uses Aeneas, a toolchain for verifying Rust programs through a functional translation to Lean, on jxl-rs, a Rust rewrite of the jpeg-xl codec. The proof shows an unchecked array access always stays within bounds and showcases recent Aeneas features, including official grind support and loop combinators.

⊢Checked against: jonathan.protzenko.fr

Lean core·May 7

Sebastian Graf merges experimental mvcgen' tactic into the Lean toolchain

The pull request adds mvcgen', reimplemented from the ground up on the new SymM-based framework for efficient symbolic evaluation. It will soon replace mvcgen and can outperform it by a factor of more than 100 on some synthetic benchmarks. Join point sharing and introduction of local specs are not yet supported.

⊢Checked against: lean4#13644

Papers and events·May 1

Leo de Moura presents machine-checked mathematics talk at Stanford symposium

Leo de Moura, Chief Architect of the Lean FRO, spoke on "Machine-Checked Mathematics in the Age of AI" at the Stanford Future of Mathematics Symposium on May 1, 2026. The slides, written in Verso, cover Mathlib's 270,000+ formalized theorems, Verso Blueprint, Comparator (a judge for Lean proofs), and Lean-based IMO medal AIs.

⊢Checked against: leodemoura.github.io

Papers and events·Mar 18

Leo de Moura presents Lean 4 at NASA Formal Methods Symposium

Leo de Moura, Chief Architect of the Lean FRO, presents "Lean 4: A Unified Platform for Programming and Verification" at the NASA Formal Methods Symposium on May 6, 2026. The talk covers grind, Lean's new proof automation, and industry verification work including Cedar, SampCert, and SymCrypt.
